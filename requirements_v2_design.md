# System design — computational pipeline for the structured-transport pattern-forming system and its inverse problem

*Produced by running the `system-design` skill on `requirements_v2_abstract.md`. Tier 3 → full procedure + framing moves + deep search (independent proposer contexts + a fresh judge with kill authority). The object designed is the **computational/methodological pipeline**: a forward simulator of the model, and a likelihood-free method that identifies θ and selects among candidate operators.*

## Triage: 3 — expensive to reverse, and the requirements arrive as solution-verbs

The requirements are stated almost entirely as mechanisms (Turing instability, adjoint sensitivity, Lyapunov functionals, likelihood-free inference, adiabatic elimination). Solution-verbs in the spec is itself a Tier-3 trigger; on top of that the deliverable is a research program's computational architecture (persisted simulation formats, an inversion pipeline, months of downstream work), so reversal cost dwarfs the cost of searching hard. ## Requirements ledger

*Outcome form. Quantities attached; `unknown — ask` names the design choice that hangs on the missing number. Challenges are logged **upward** as questions — none is silently applied.*

**Forward-model requirements**

- **R1 (non-negotiable): the attractor is spatially patterned, generated by the coupling.** Finite-wavelength band k ∈ (k₁,k₂); pattern arises from the u–w short-range-activation / long-range-inhibition coupling, **not** from structure in φ; under a tilted potential the instability is convective and the pattern drifts; post-onset branch structure is multistable / history-dependent. — *quantities:* wavelength band; domain size L vs wavelength λ (how many wavelengths across Ω → grid resolution); drift speed at max tilt; number of coexisting branches — **all unknown — ask** (set grid resolution and continuation strategy).
- **R2: response depends on the forcing *distribution*, and coarse forcing maps to resolved effective input.** The non-smooth gate opens a Jensen gap: E𝒫[g(input)] ≠ g(E𝒫[input]); redistribution is a downscaling operator fixed by (φ,Θ). — *quantities:* forcing spatial scale vs domain resolution; threshold Θ; event rate / intermittency / tail heaviness of 𝒫 (sets the Jensen-gap size) — **unknown — ask**.
- **R3 (challenged upward): carry internal structure only to the depth the outputs read.** *Challenge:* "Do any required outputs — the state functionals, the P5 condition, the θ-map — read more than one or two low moments of n? If only u and a moment or two are read, the a-transport closes to a small moment system and the internal coordinate need not be a resolved dimension. If the full n(·,a,·) shape is genuinely required, name the functional that needs it." — *quantities:* number of moments any requirement reads; range/resolution of a if it must be resolved — **unknown — ask**. **Load-bearing for per-run cost.**
- **R4 (challenged upward): fast field may be slaved.** *Challenge:* "May the production forward map solve w's quasi-steady gated balance given u, rather than time-integrate the ε-fast w? Does any target need the ε-fast transient of w, as opposed to its slaved value? R6's transient targets are slow-manifold rates, below the ε timescale." — *quantities:* ε; observation timescale vs ε — **unknown — ask**. **Load-bearing for per-run cost.**

**Inverse-problem requirements**

- **R5 (challenged upward): recover θ; cost scales with its *effective* dimension.** θ is shared across Ω (φ, transport coefficients, local forcing law fixed from data); nominal p ≈ 50 but with functional interdependencies lowering the effective dimension; the θ→behaviour map is over-determined (constraints ≫ effective free params), not an interpolant. *Challenge:* "What is d_eff after the interdependencies? Cost scales with d_eff, not 50; if d_eff ≲ 10 the design differs sharply from p = 50." — *quantities:* d_eff; number of independent observational constraints — **unknown — ask**. **Load-bearing for run count.**
- **R6: attractor and transient, autonomous and non-autonomous; transient targets are rates.** Must use both the attractor and transients, under autonomous and drifting 𝒫 where the system is dragged through the R1 onset; the central non-autonomous question is **tracking vs rate-induced transition**; targets are relaxation/return rate, distance to onset, critical slowing down. — *quantities:* forcing drift rate vs slow relaxation rate; number/length of transient records; how close to onset the data sit — **unknown — ask**.
- **R7: self-consistency, not naive regression (correctness constraint).** A distinguished θ (or θ-field) is picked by the stationarity condition ∂Λ/∂θ′|_{θ′=θ} = 0, where Λ is the growth rate of a θ′-shifted perturbation evaluated in the field produced by the attractor of the base-θ system — self-consistent, because that field is generated by the dynamics at θ. Load-bearing for inversion: the covariate that would "explain" θ(x) (R2's effective-input field) **is itself a functional of the state** (endogenous), so regressing θ on it is **circular**; the only non-circular constraint is the self-consistency fixed point, which **equals** the stationarity condition at the attractor.
- **R8: distribution over θ + model-selection verdict; no tractable likelihood.** The forward map runs through the R1 bifurcation with history-dependent branch selection and stochastic forcing → no closed-form likelihood → identification is likelihood-free / simulation-based. Output: a distribution over θ + a verdict among a **small** candidate-operator set. **The joint constraint from more than one independent functional must pin θ, and that joint pinning is what licenses extrapolation beyond the sampled forcing** (vs interpolation across sampled conditions). *Machinery (ABC vs neural SBI vs …) is out of scope.* — *quantities:* number of candidate operators; required posterior/UQ fidelity; extrapolation reach — **unknown — ask**.
- **R9 (challenged upward): generality across instantiations.** The spec never names what u is and claims generality is "close to free." *Challenge:* "How many *distinct* instantiations will actually be run (witnesses)? Generalize only over witnesses. If one concrete system exists today and others are rumored, the reversible default is to build for the one behind a thin operator seam and retrofit when a second lands. If ≥ 2 are committed now, name them." — *quantity:* count of committed instantiations — **unknown — ask**.

**Challenge to the document's own scarcity claim (§4), logged upward:** "§4 frames the binding resource as sensitivity *conditioning* (adjoint vs tangent). I believe that is off the critical path and is inherited scarcity: a likelihood-free inversion (R8) is largely gradient-free in θ, and the only gradient P5 truly needs is of a single scalar Λ in a modest-p space (§4 half-admits this — 'the adjoint's p-independence is nearly moot'). Confirm the binding resource is instead the forward-evaluation budget below?"

### Scarce resource

The binding resource is the **forward-simulator evaluation budget = N (runs to attractor) × C (cost per run)**. **N** is forced by R8's likelihood-free demand and scales with the *effective* dimension d_eff (R5), not nominal p ≈ 50; **R7's circularity is what locks these runs onto the critical path** — because the only non-circular constraint is the self-consistency fixed point, cheap one-shot regression cannot replace simulation. **C** is dominated by two *reducible* factors — resolving the fast field w over ε ≪ 1 (R4) and the internal coordinate a (R3) — sitting on an *irreducible* floor set by run-to-attractor time diverging near onset (R6 critical slowing down) and mandatory 𝒫-averaging for the Jensen gap (R2). Sensitivity conditioning near onset is a numerical **hazard to contain**, not the resource being spent.

## The floor

**Design.** One 2-D grid; the literal stiff multiscale PDE — time-integrate ε∂ₜw (Δt ~ ε), resolve n(x,y,a) on a 3-D grid, u = ∫n da; run to the attractor past onset; drive whatever likelihood-free engine with one fresh full-fidelity run per proposed θ, each candidate operator run separately.

**Verdict — fails, but honestly: partly by structure, partly pending the challenged numbers.** It is *correct*; it breaks on the scarce resource. Structural part (holds regardless of the unknowns): **ε ≪ 1 is spec** (R4), so stiffness forces Δt ~ ε and step count ~ T_obs/ε **whatever** d_eff or the run count turn out to be; R6's critical slowing down drives T_obs → ∞ near onset; and R8 × R7 make the run count a product (posterior draws × operators × fixed-point iterations × forcing conditions). Number-dependent part: the exact per-run cost N_x·N_y·N_a·(T_obs/ε) and the exact N are gated by R3/R4/R5 (unknown — ask). So the floor cannot be dismissed by arithmetic alone — but the two factors it inflates (per-run cost via a and ε; count via p vs d_eff) are reducible cheaply and reversibly, which is what the winner does. The floor does **not** justify resolving a or integrating w; nothing in the ledger witnesses those.

## Candidates (deep search — one framing move each; A = first thought)

- **A [first thought] — build it as written.** *Commitment:* every state variable the spec names is a resolved axis advanced by its own operator (first-class a-axis; w time-integrated at ε); no reduction anywhere. *Pays for:* R1/R2/R6/R7/R8 in principle, and R3-hard/R4-hard *if* they hold. *Costs:* per-run N_x·N_y·N_a·(T_obs/ε), × N_sims — the two most expensive commitments multiply. *Wins when:* a functional reads the full n(·,a,·) shape (R3 hard) **or** a target needs the ε-fast w transient (R4 hard); and, unconditionally, as the one-time reference oracle any reduction must be validated against.
- **B — slow-manifold reduction (move 4 + reduction).** *Commitment:* production evolves only the slow manifold — a small fixed moment vector M=(m₀…m_K) on the 2-D grid, w slaved to its gated quasi-steady balance — so no ε-fast transient and no a-axis is ever resolved in production; resolved implementations exist only behind typed seams. *Pays for:* the **per-run multiplicand** (removes the 1/ε and N_a factors — the factor ε≪1 makes unconditionally binding), and discharges R2 with the same object (the slaved-w solve *is* the (φ,Θ) downscaling / Jensen functional). *Costs:* seam twins + a certification stage + a cache; wrong (not merely coarse) if an output reads a discarded DOF; the gated quasi-steady solve is a hard complementarity problem. *Wins when:* ε genuinely small, outputs read low moments, and forcing is dense per relaxation time (event-rate × ε ≫ 1).
- **C — record → replay (move 2).** *Commitment:* the forward map is never called, only replayed from records keyed by a validity `ContextKey`, with a live correction restoring self-consistency. *Pays for:* the **run count** (the (φ,Θ) downscaling and the finite branch set are recorded once, replayed across many 𝒫). *Costs:* two names (Atlas, ContextKey); pure overhead when the discrete context changes within an evaluation. *Wins when:* autonomous-dominant, sparse bifurcations, few branches, scalar Θ, d_eff ≲ 10, large N_sim.
- **D — amortized surrogate + honest guard (moves 7 + 5).** *Commitment:* the forcing→functionals map is smooth/single-valued/low-d on the query-mass majority, and every failing region is cheaply detectable a priori, so a fast answer for a hard query is never *expressible*. *Pays for:* the **run count** (bulk draws hit a microsecond emulator; operationalizes R5's d_eff). *Costs:* two names (Emulator, Gate) + a training asset; worst exactly at the load-bearing places (onset, multistability, extrapolation), where it must defer. *Wins when:* d_eff genuinely small, many repeated draws × operators × conditions × witnesses, **and** a non-trivial fraction of informative queries sits far from onset.

**Winner: B**, as the load-bearing spine of a hybrid (details below). **Eliminations:**
- **A** — its distinguishing mechanisms (resolved a, integrated w) are justified only by R3-hard/R4-hard, but the ledger names no functional reading full n(·,a,·) (R3) and R6's targets are "slow-manifold rates, below the ε timescale" (R4); it pays N_a·(T_obs/ε) per run for a regime the ledger excludes. Kept only as B's offline validator.
- **C** — its "never call the forward map" commitment needs records reusable across θ, but R6 *centers* the non-autonomous rate-tipping regime where C concedes it runs "worse than the floor," and R7 (endogenous covariate) + R1 (history-dependent branches) deny any context slower than the per-evaluation state. Its `BranchAtlas` + cache survive as commitment-free grafts.
- **D** — amortization is conditional on unknowns (d_eff small, N_query large — R5/R8) *and* structurally mis-targeted: the informative queries (R7 fixed point, R6 near-onset critical slowing down) fall in its *deferred* region, so it accelerates the cheap far-from-onset bulk and defers the expensive landing. It degrades *to* the winner rather than beating it — so it is held as the winner's first accelerator, not a rival.

## The commitment

**Production evolves only the slow manifold: a small fixed moment vector M = (m₀…m_K) on the 2-D grid, with w slaved to its threshold-gated quasi-steady balance — so the ε-fast transient and the internal coordinate a are never resolved in production.** This is the one commitment; it attacks the per-run multiplicand the ledger makes *unconditionally* binding (ε ≪ 1 is spec). The Emulator's smoothness claim (D) and the reusable-record claim (C) are deliberately **not** adopted as commitments — they are dimension-/regime-gated accelerators that attach only when the unknowns permit. One commitment stays one.

**Kept true by structure (not developer discipline):**
- **State type.** `SlowState = { M: Field[K+1] on Grid2D }` — there is no w-history field and no a-axis in the production state type. w is returned by `WField.evaluate(M, forcingLaw, θ)` and consumed immediately, never stored. Resolving the transient or the a-profile requires a *different type* / *different simulator class*, so you cannot drift into it.
- **Typed seams for the excluded branches.** `WField ∈ {SlavedW, RelaxedW}`, `AProcess ∈ {MomentClosure, ResolvedA}`, selected at construction. Production wires `{SlavedW, MomentClosure}`; flipping to a resolved witness is one line — which *is* R9's thin operator seam.
- **R7 anti-circularity, enforced.** The endogenous effective-input field is private to `WField` and appears on no public interface; the only θ-surface the inference can reach is `stationarity(θ) → ∂Λ/∂θ′` evaluated at the attractor. The circular regression R7 forbids is structurally unreachable.
- **Output-contract invariant (imported from D as a hard rule, independent of any emulator).** The forward map's public result type is a **vector of ≥ 2 independent state functionals across the required forcing conditions**, never a scalar. A scalar output is unrepresentable, so θ cannot be silently under-pinned — this is what licenses R8's extrapolation-by-joint-pinning rather than interpolation.

## Kill question

*The single assumption whose falsity makes this design unnecessary:* **there is a genuine fast–slow separation and the outputs live on the slow manifold** — concretely ε ≪ 1, forcing dense per relaxation time (event-rate × ε ≫ 1), and outputs reading only low moments.

*Argued against, from ledger facts only:* ε ≪ 1 is **given** (R4, spec — only its magnitude is unknown), so the separation is not a hope; R6's transient targets are stated to be slow-manifold rates below ε; R3/R8's functionals are low-order and R7's Λ is a scalar. The only genuinely open sub-assumption is **event-rate × ε ≫ 1** (R2 intermittency × R4 ε): if forcing events are sparse relative to w's relaxation, each event is a resolved transient and slaving breaks. That single open case is routed through the `RelaxedW` seam and a low-rate certification witness, not assumed away. **Verdict: survives** — the commitment holds wherever ε ≪ 1 and forcing is not pathologically sparse, and the one exception is a typed seam, not a redesign.

## What survives deletion

- `SlowIntegrator` → R1 (no attractor), R6 (no transients). Keep.
- `SlavedW` / `WField` → R1 (coupling), R2 (the downscaling / Jensen functional *is* this solve). Keep. `RelaxedW` twin → R4's other branch; dormant, cheap. Keep.
- `MomentClosure` / `AProcess` → all of R1–R8 (u is the field). Keep. `ResolvedA` twin → R3's other branch; dormant. Keep.
- `ForcingLaw` (sample the law, drive the gate) → R2, R6. Keep.
- `stationarity` probe (Λ, ∂Λ/∂θ′ at the attractor) → R7, R6 (rates, critical slowing down). Keep.
- **`FunctionalVector` (≥ 2 independent functionals × conditions)** → R5 (over-determination), R7 (fixed point), R8 (joint pinning → extrapolation). Keep — the piece the whole proposer set under-specified.
- `OperatorSeam` → R8 (candidate set) + R9 (retrofit). Keep, thin.
- `Certification` (the resolved A-run as oracle) → discharges the R3/R4 *challenges*; without it the reduction is asserted, not shown. Keep — offline, not in the hot loop.
- `SimBank` + `BranchAtlas` (from C) → **no correctness line breaks; only cost rises.** Survive on economics (R8's many runs) — the honest mark of a compute↔storage trade. Deletable if runs are cheap/few.
- `Gate` + `Emulator` (from D) → in always-defer mode the Gate *is* the winner (identity); it earns its place only when the go/no-go unknowns (R5 d_eff small, R8 many draws, R6 informative mass off-onset) resolve favorably. Dormant by default; **no second commitment** until then.

## What this settles

- The ε-fast transient is **never computed** in production, and the a-coordinate is **never allocated** — the two factors that make the floor's per-run cost break are removed by construction, not by tuning.
- The endogenous effective-input field **cannot be used as a regressor** (it is not on any public interface), so R7's circular one-shot fit is not expressible — the inversion is forced onto the self-consistency fixed point.
- A **scalar-only** forward output is unrepresentable, so θ cannot be silently under-pinned; extrapolation is licensed only by joint multi-functional agreement (R8).
- Model selection and future instantiations (R8/R9) enter through **one** thin operator seam; no per-operator rewrite.

---

## What this makes hard (and how we'd cope)

- **ε-fast phenomena / full a-profile** — production returns a *wrong* answer, not a coarse one. Cope: the typed seam (`RelaxedW` / `ResolvedA`) for the specific witness; `Certification` flags it if such a functional is in the cert set.
- **Sparse forcing (event-rate × ε ≲ 1)** — slaving invalid. Cope: detect from `ForcingLaw` + ε; route that witness to `RelaxedW`.
- **Near-onset conditioning (irreducible floor)** — run-to-attractor time diverges (R6 critical slowing down) and 𝒫-averaging (R2) is mandatory; the reduction does **not** touch these. Cope: this is where the count-reduction upgrades (Gate/atlas) and a robust semismooth-Newton solve for the gated balance earn their keep; report distance-to-onset as a first-class diagnostic.
- **Non-autonomous drift through onset (R6)** — the forcing is a trajectory and tipping is history-dependent; a static-forcing emulator cannot cover it. Cope: non-autonomous runs are first-class true-simulator; any accelerator only speeds the autonomous baseline they are compared against, so the tracking-vs-tipping verdict is always a mechanism result.

## Kill condition (each losing candidate's "wins when", preserved as a handoff trigger)

- **→ A** the moment a required output functional reads the full internal distribution n(·,a,·) (R3 resolves hard) **or** a named target lives below ε and needs the fast w-transient (R4 resolves hard). Also, unconditionally, A remains the one-time reference oracle B is validated against.
- **→ C** if analyses become autonomous-dominant with sparse bifurcations, few finite branches, scalar Θ and d_eff ≲ 10 at large N_sim — i.e. R6's rate-induced tipping is demoted from central to peripheral.
- **→ D (train the dormant Gate's Emulator)** if d_eff is genuinely small (≈ 6, R5), with 3–4 operators (R8), a 10³–10⁴-draw posterior × several conditions × ≥ 2 witnesses (R6/R9), **and** a non-trivial fraction of informative queries sits far from onset (R6).

**Load-bearing unknowns, ranked by how much they move the winner** (these are the questions to answer before building):
1. **R3 — moment count K / does any functional or kernel read more than 1–2 low moments of n?** If the full n-shape is read, *every* reduced design flips and A wins. (Most complete flip.)
2. **R4 — ε magnitude (and observation timescale vs ε).** ε = O(1) ⇒ no separation ⇒ the winner's reduction evaporates (revert toward the floor / explicit w).
3. **R5 — effective dimension d_eff.** ≲ 10 makes the count-reduction upgrades (Gate, atlas) worth attaching; ≳ 20–50 kills the amortizers and strains even the winner.
4. **R2 × R4 — event-rate × ε.** ≲ 1 flips the forward-map seam `SlavedW` → `RelaxedW` for that witness.
5. **R6 — how close to onset the data sit (and drift rate vs relaxation rate).** Sets the irreducible per-run floor and caps any accelerator's payoff.

## The design

**Forward simulator (production).** Method-of-lines on a 2-D grid over Ω sized to resolve several pattern wavelengths (R1: L/λ — ask). State is `SlowState = { M: Field[K+1] }` (R3-closed; K fixed at the highest moment any rate/kernel reads). `WField = SlavedW` solves w's threshold-gated quasi-steady balance given M and the sampled forcing (R4-slaved) — this solve simultaneously realizes R2's (φ,Θ) downscaling and the Jensen gap, so there is no separate downscaling module. `ForcingLaw` samples the intermittent law 𝒫 (autonomous or drifting) and drives the gate. Integrate the reduced slow field to the attractor; read the `FunctionalVector` (≥ 2 independent functionals across the required conditions) and, via the tangent-linear equation with Benettin renormalization at the attractor, the growth rate Λ and its θ′-gradient.

**Inverse method.** The self-consistency solver is a root-find of `stationarity(θ) = ∂Λ/∂θ′|_{θ′=θ} = 0` evaluated at the attractor (R7), calling only the forward map — a fixed-point solve, never a one-shot regression, with the endogenous covariate structurally hidden. The chosen (out-of-scope) likelihood-free engine consumes the `FunctionalVector` to return a distribution over θ and a model-selection verdict across the `OperatorSeam`'s small candidate set (R8); joint agreement of the multiple functionals at an out-of-envelope point is the licensing check for extrapolation.

**Offline / dormant scaffolding.** `Certification` runs the resolved-A / explicit-w implementations (candidate A's build, behind the seams) once to bound the slaving + closure error and discharge the R3/R4 challenges. `SimBank` (+ C's `BranchAtlas`) banks the cheap runs keyed by `(operator, forcing-summary, θ)` and enumerates the finite branch set to supply the R8 candidate set and warm-starts — economics only, deletable. `Gate` wraps the forward map behind a `Served | Deferred` sum type and defaults to **always-defer** (≡ the plain winner); its `Emulator` is trained only if the ranked unknowns above resolve into D's win-region, at which point bulk draws are emulator-served and the informative near-onset landing auto-defers to the true simulator — an accelerator that can never silently answer a hard query, and that adds no second commitment.

**Two decisions a reader must internalize; the rest follows:** (1) *production lives on the slow manifold* (gives the state type, the seams, the certification oracle); (2) *the forward map's output is an irreducible multi-functional vector evaluated at the self-consistency fixed point* (gives the inversion's correctness and R8's extrapolation licence). Everything else is consequence or dormant, priced scaffolding.
